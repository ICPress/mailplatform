Article Removed

One of your published articles has been removed from ICPress following an editorial review. This notice is issued to inform you of the decision and the standards against which the content was assessed.

ICPress publishes reporting on consequential world events and holds all contributions to the standards expected of independent, responsible journalism. The integrity of the platform depends on every contributor upholding those standards.

Editorial Standards — Common Grounds for Removal

Accuracy & Verification
All published claims must be verifiable and substantiated. Unconfirmed assertions, rumours, and factual errors cannot be permitted to stand on record.
Sourcing & Attribution
Sources must be credible and properly credited. Quotes, data, and visual material require explicit attribution.
Originality
Submitted work must be original. Reproduction of another's reporting or analysis without attribution constitutes plagiarism and is prohibited.
Impartiality & Fairness
Reporting must be measured and fair. Content that targets individuals or groups on discriminatory grounds will not be published.
Editorial Integrity
Fabricated quotes, manufactured sources, and misleading headlines undermine the credibility of the platform and will result in immediate removal.

The full editorial guidelines are available on the ICPress website. We encourage you to review them before submitting further work. Repeated violations may result in suspension of contributor privileges.
